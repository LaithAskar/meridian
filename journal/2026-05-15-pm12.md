### State of 2.1 (verified this run)

`backtest/signals/quant.py` implements `QuantStrategy(Strategy)`:

- **History accumulation**: buffers OHLCV rows per symbol using capitalised keys (`Close`, `Open`, etc.) for backend-function compatibility. Capped at 300 bars per call to bound per-bar compute — at the end of a 14-year backtest (≈ 3 528 trading days), this keeps per-bar work O(300) instead of O(T), a ~10× speedup.
- **Warmup gate**: emits no signals until `min_history_bars` (default 60) bars have accumulated — prevents the engine from trading on an under-specified cross-sectional ranking.
- **Signal pipeline**: `momentum_signals` (cross-sectional ranking + time-series + EMA crossover + RSI) → `mean_reversion_signals` (RSI extremes + Bollinger Band position + z-score) → `aggregate_signals` with regime-adjusted weights. Factor signals are empty throughout (DESIGN.md: no factor model).
- **Regime detection**: offline `_regime_at(ts)` replicates `detect_regime` logic using pre-loaded SPY/VIX series, with no live network calls. Only data up to and including `ts` is used — no look-ahead.
- **Order generation**: sells first (exit positions with aggregate sell signal), then buys highest-conviction signals into open slots, equal-weighted at `equity / max_positions`, skipping symbols already held and skipping buys that exceed available cash.
